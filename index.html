Get More from Your Self-Study with AI

Want to get more out of your French self-study time? The AI tools available in your course can help you prepare, practice, review, and get extra support when you need it.

Prepare & Practice

Site Companion

When using the course site in Google Chrome, look for Ask Gemini. Use the Site Companion while preparing for your next lesson. You can ask questions about the lesson content, vocabulary, grammar, or concepts you would like to understand better.

You can choose how you want to interact with Gemini. Use Ask Gemini for a written conversation, or switch to Gemini Live for a real-time spoken interaction. Gemini Live lets you have a more natural back-and-forth conversation, which can be especially useful when you want to practice speaking.

Interactive Practice

Preparing for a C’est à vous activity? To practice a role-play or receive feedback on homework, you can use Ask Gemini on that page of the core site or IL site, and start Gemini Live for an interactive experience. You can describe the situation, ask it to take one of the roles, and rehearse the conversation before class.

You can also ask questions such as:

  • 💬 "Why do we use this form here and not this one?"
  • 💬 "Can you explain this grammar point in a simpler way?"
  • 💬 "Can you give me another example?"

Think of the Site Companion as an extra resource you can use while working through your lesson materials.

Pronunciation & Support

Speak Easy Lab & Ask Lumi

When you are working on pronunciation, open Speak Easy Lab.

  • Start with Learn to review pronunciation rules and examples.
  • Move to Practice to listen, repeat, and record yourself.

If something comes up while you are practicing, Ask Lumi is there to help.

  • ✨ Having trouble pronouncing a word? Ask Lumi for a tip.
  • ✨ Not sure why a sound changes? Ask about the rule.
  • ✨ Grammar or vocab questions? You can ask Lumi that too.

Lumi is available throughout the lessons, so support is only a question away.

Conversation

VERA Partner

Want more opportunities to actually use your French in conversation? Practice with VERA, your AI conversational partner.

VERA gives you opportunities to engage in extended French conversations and practice responding naturally in real time. Use it to build speaking fluency, listening comprehension, and confidence through meaningful conversation.

You can practice everyday situations, discuss different topics, or simply challenge yourself to keep a conversation going in French. VERA adapts to the interaction and can provide support when you need help expressing an idea.

The goal is simple: listen more, speak more, and become more comfortable sustaining a conversation in French.
Review & Create

NotebookLM

You can also use NotebookLM to turn your course materials into additional study opportunities.

Use Cases

  • Upload a grammar PDF or course material and ask NotebookLM to create practice exercises, quiz you, or explain a difficult section.
  • Upload grammar explanations from the "Did You Notice?" sections to create interactive practice with immediate feedback.

Because NotebookLM works from the sources you provide, it can help you interact with your course materials in a different way instead of simply reading through them page by page.

Make AI Part of Your Practice

You do not need to use every tool every time. Choose the tool that fits what you are trying to do:

  • Prepare with the Site Companion.
  • Practice speaking with Gemini Live.
  • Practice pronunciation with Speak Easy Lab.
  • Ask Lumi when you need support.
  • Build fluency with VERA.
  • Review & create with NotebookLM.

Use these tools to make your independent learning time more active, focused, and personalized.
